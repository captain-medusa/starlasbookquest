You know how hard it can be to picture a beloved character when reading a book, right? I’ve definitely had my share of mental blocks, and I often end up searching for fan art to help bring them to life. (Though, I’ll admit, I’ve accidentally spoiled a few surprises doing that in the past!)

Fortunately, the fan art I’m sharing below is spoiler-free, and I’m thrilled to introduce you to one of my favorite cozy sci-fi space crews. Can you match each character with their description? Just a heads-up, the descriptions come from the books, but these fan arts are the artists' interpretations—so they’re not 100% true to the text.

<span class="highlight">In my favorite piece, Cain has a beard, Robin is wearing his glasses, and Marirose is in a yellow top.</span>

<span class="highlight">I’ve changed the characters’ names so that, put in the correct order, they reveal the name of their author.</span>
